# CTR Decompilation — Structure / Type Tracking

Tracks structures, enums, and globals recovered in the Ghidra project. Reuse and refine
existing structures before creating new ones (see `CLAUDE.md`). Pre-existing WIP Ghidra
structures must be re-validated against actual usage before being trusted.

> **⚠️ TOOLING GOTCHA (learned 2026-06-24):** `remove_struct_field` on `struct Driver` (and likely
> any non-packed fixed-size struct here) **REPACKS** — it deletes the field's bytes and shifts every
> subsequent field UP, shrinking the struct and putting all later fields at the WRONG binary offset
> (silent corruption of every consumer's decompile). There is no internal "insert" primitive
> (`add_struct_field` at an occupied offset OVERWRITES; it only extends past the current end), and
> `run_script_inline` (which has `Structure.insertAtOffset`) is DISABLED. To GROW a field in place,
> `modify_struct_field` only works if the following bytes are already undefined. **Do NOT remove a
> middle field to make room** — recovery requires peeling the entire tail (remove from the end
> backward) and re-appending every field with exact cumulative offsets. The bot-list-node typing
> (`unk598`+`unk59c` → `struct Item`) was ABANDONED for this reason. After a peel/re-append, the
> auto-prefixer re-prefixes the restored fields (e.g. `botFlags`→`nBotFlags`, `botNavFrame`→
> `pBotNavFrame`, `maskObj`→`pMaskObj`, `ai_progress_cooldown`→`nAi_progress_cooldown`) — cosmetic
> only; binary offsets are correct (Driver re-verified at exactly 1592 bytes).
